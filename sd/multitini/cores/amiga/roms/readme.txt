Supply your own legally obtained A600-compatible Kickstart ROM as kick.rom
in multitini/cores/amiga/roms/ on the SD card. No Amiga ROM is supplied.
Tested: A600 Kickstart 3.1, revision 40.063 (512 KiB).
For Amiga 1200 AGA PAL, place a user-owned A1200 Kickstart 3.1 ROM
in the same directory as kick-a1200.rom.
The loader accepts exact 256 KiB, 512 KiB or 1 MiB files and mirrors smaller
images across its 1 MiB ROM aperture. File size alone does not prove compatibility.
